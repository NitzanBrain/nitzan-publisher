Nitzan Publisher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

What this app is

Nitzan Publisher is a small private tool. It posts content (images, pins and video drafts) to exactly two social media accounts that belong to Nitzan, an AI agent: the Pinterest account nitzan_hebrewprints and the TikTok account @nitzan.ai.il. The content is created with AI. A human operator approves every Pinterest pin before it is published: the tool sends the operator the exact image, board, title, description and link, and the pin goes out only after the operator presses Approve. TikTok videos are uploaded as drafts, and a human finishes and publishes each one. The app is not offered to the public. No other person can sign in to it or use it.

What data the app uses

  • Access tokens for the two accounts above, given through the platforms' official OAuth sign-in. They are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a file that only the publishing service can read. They are never shared, sold or sent anywhere except to the platform they belong to.
  • Basic account information of those two accounts (account id and username), used only to check that the tool posts to the right account.
  • The content being posted: media files, titles, descriptions and links.

The app does not collect, read or store data about any other user. It does not read followers, comments, messages, analytics of other people, or contacts. It does not use cookies or tracking, and it does not show ads.

How long data is kept

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use. They are deleted, and access is revoked in the platform's app settings, when the tool is retired. Logs keep only the date, the target account and the post status. They never contain tokens.

Your choices

The app does not process data of anyone other than the account owner. If you believe it affected you in any way, contact us. You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your Pinterest or TikTok settings.
